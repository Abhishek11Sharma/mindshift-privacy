MIND//SHIFT

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026 · App version 0.2.0 · Package com.mind.shift.game

In short: MIND//SHIFT does not collect, send or share any personal data. It works fully offline and asks for no Android permissions.

What the app stores

Your game progress is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Best score and best streak
  • Lifetime totals (sessions, rounds, accuracy, rule shifts detected)
  • Your last 20 sessions
  • Your settings (sound on/off, whether the how-to was shown)

This data never leaves your device. The developer cannot see it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No internet access: the app does not request the INTERNET permission
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or third-party SDKs
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files
  • No in-app purchases

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions.

Children

Because the app collects no data of any kind, it collects none from children. It contains no ads, links out or social features.

Your choices and deletion

  • Open Settings → Reset progress in the app to delete your saved scores and history.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change before the new version is released.

Contact
